A SIMPLE FRAMEWORK

The CLASS prompting framework

Give AI the information a teacher already knows. Clearer context produces more useful output.

CContext

Set the scene.

  • Who are the pupils?
  • Subject, year and topic?
  • Prior knowledge?
  • Constraints?
LLearning

Be clear on the purpose.

  • What is the objective?
  • What should pupils know, understand or do?
AActivity

Specify what you need.

  • Type of resource or task?
  • Duration and format?
  • Specific requirements?
SSupport

Include key considerations.

  • Scaffolds and challenge?
  • SEND, EAL or PP?
  • Resources or constraints?
SSuccess

Define what good looks like.

  • Required output?
  • How will you judge quality?
  • Retrieval or assessment?
THREE PRACTICAL DEMOS

From vague requests to useful teaching materials

1 · Better brief, better output
VAGUE PROMPT

“Create a lesson on photosynthesis.”

IMPROVED CLASS PROMPT

Year 8 Science. Explain photosynthesis using the word equation. Use explicit teaching, guided practice and an independent task. Address the “food from soil” misconception, support weaker readers and finish with a five-question exit ticket.

RESULT: A detailed, relevant lesson plan you can use.
2 · Improve, do not regenerate
ORIGINAL ACTIVITY

“Read the information about the causes of World War One and answer questions 1–8.”

IMPROVEMENT PROMPT

“The objective is to explain which cause was most significant. Suggest three ways to improve this activity without changing the learning objective.”

RESULT: Better activities that increase thinking and engagement.
3 · Make something for tomorrow
PROMPT 1

Year 7 Maths: list five likely misconceptions about negative numbers with diagnostic questions.

PROMPT 2

Turn the best three into multiple-choice hinge questions with plausible distractors.

PROMPT 3

Create a five-question exit ticket aligned to the same objective.

RESULT: A focused assessment sequence.
READY TO USE

Prompt bank and quick fixes

Prompt bank

Lesson planCreate a 50-minute lesson for Year [ ] on [topic] with explicit teaching, guided practice, independent practice, differentiation and an exit ticket.
ImproveSuggest three ways to improve this activity without changing the learning objective: [paste activity].
MisconceptionsList likely misconceptions about [topic] and give a diagnostic question for each.
Hinge QsCreate five multiple-choice hinge questions on [topic] with plausible distractors.
ScaffoldingScaffold this task for lower-attaining pupils without reducing the thinking.
Exit ticketCreate a five-minute exit ticket aligned to this objective: [objective].

Quick fixes

Too generic?Make this more specific to my context: [add year group, topic, students, etc.].
Too long?Keep this, but make it more concise.
Need detail?Add timings, differentiation and full resources.
Different format?Convert this into a PowerPoint, worksheet or knowledge organiser.
Not quite right?Improve this, focusing on [specific element].
Try againKeep the good ideas, but improve accuracy and relevance to my context.
